## Key Principles (All Plans)

1. **Balanced > Stars & Scrubs** — 0 of 13 NFBC auction winners had Ohtani. Spread the wealth.
2. **Nail the $2-$5 buys** — depth wins H2H weeks. The mid/low tier is where leagues are won.
3. **Pitching is KING in our scoring** — Skubal (703 fpts) and Skenes (680 fpts) outscore every hitter except Ohtani/Judge. IP (+2.25), QS (+1.5), K (+1.5), SV (+5) all stack up.
4. **Overpay $5-7 on foundation guys** — make it up on the back end.
5. **Have multiple plans and pivot** — if Plan A's hinge player goes too high, shift to B/C/D.

## Nomination Strategy (All Plans)

### First 10 Picks
1. **Nominate your Plan A hinge player** early to get price discovery (Skubal for Plan A)
2. **Heat check:** Throw out a Closer 4-7 or Catcher 4 to gauge room spending
3. **If you land a top SP:** Immediately nominate remaining top SPs to force opponents to pay $40+

### Mid-Auction
4. **If top-3 in remaining $:** Rapid-fire nominate your targets
5. **If low on funds:** Nominate players you can't afford to drain the room

### Endgame
6. **Dollar days:** Nominate players you want at $1
7. **Study opponents' max bids and roster needs** — sneak players through when all $2+ bidders have a position filled
8. **Throw out $3-$5 players you DON'T want** before nominating your actual targets
